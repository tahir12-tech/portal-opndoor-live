Running your book

The management view of the portal: analytics across everything you are responsible for, the guarantee fee and your commission, your monthly statement, exports, and managing your people.

What you can see

Where a referrer sees only their own referrals, management sees everything. At an agency that is every office and every referrer in it; at a supplier it is every agency, office and referrer in your company. You can add and manage your own people, and every tracking and reporting screen adds up across the whole of it.

Records opndoor owns, and the portal's own settings, are opndoor's to change. Ask your account manager.

Reporting

The Sent, Paid, Deed issued funnel, the volume charts and the breakdowns all add up across your book, and every one of them follows the period selector at the top, from the last 7 days through to all time. A table that has been cut to a period says so, so a part-month figure is never mistaken for a lifetime total.

The guarantee fee and your commission

The guarantee fee is the fee agreed with your agency, usually one month's rent. Some agreements price it in weeks instead. A joint tenancy is priced once and split between the tenants, and each tenant pays their own share.

Your commission is set out in your agreement and shown on your Commission tab and monthly statement.

Referrers never see commission
  • Commission figures are shown to Directors, to Management and to opndoor.
  • A Manager sees everything else, but not what the agency earns.
  • A referrer's own performance export has every commission line and column left out of it.

Your monthly statement

Commission runs on a monthly cycle. Each statement covers the previous calendar month, is worked out after refunds, and is paid on the 15th of the month after that: September's is paid on 15 October. The statement is emailed when the month is posted, and the same documents are on your Commission tab.

A month you can see before it has been posted is a draft. It says so, and the figures can still move.

Exports

  • Performance. Your reporting for the chosen period, as a branded workbook. A referrer's copy has no commission in it.
  • Applications. The records behind whatever you are looking at.

Agencies and offices

On the Agencies screen you can open any agency or office and see the referrals, the fees and the people behind it. Click any figure to go straight to the applications it is counting.

If you work at a supplier, you can also add an agency or an office here, and your people can add one while they refer. opndoor checks a new record afterwards for duplicates. At an agency opndoor has onboarded, opndoor sets up the offices.

Your people

On Team you can invite a colleague and set what they can see. From any person's row:

  • Send password reset. Emails them a link to set a new password.
  • Reset two-factor. Clears their authenticator so they enrol a new one next time they sign in. It is recorded.
  • Change level. Moves somebody between levels.
  • Notifications. Which emails this person is copied on.
  • Remove access. They can no longer sign in. Their referrals stay exactly as they are, and their name stays on them.

Somebody at your own level is not yours to change. Their row says so, and your account manager can do it.

The League

The full League ranks every agency, office, referrer and supplier, sorted by whichever measure you choose and paged through in full. Any row opens the applications behind it. Whether your referrers can see each other's rankings is a setting opndoor holds; ask your account manager to change it, because it changes what every referrer sees.

Finding applications

The Applications screen gives you two filters a referrer does not have:

  • Referrer. Narrow to one person's applications.
  • Period. The same periods as Reporting, counted on the date the referral was sent, with the status chips recounting to match.

They work alongside the status, agency, office and search filters, and the links from Reporting, the League and the Agencies screen arrive with them already set.
