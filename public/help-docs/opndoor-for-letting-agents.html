A failed reference doesn't have to be a lost let

When an applicant fails referencing, the deal usually falls through. opndoor lets the tenancy go ahead and protects the landlord.

Why offer it

A Deed of Guarantee in the landlord's favour

opndoor provides a Deed of Guarantee covering up to 12 months' rent, capped at £120,000, plus £10,000 of legal costs. The landlord, or you on their behalf, can claim under it. They end up better protected than on a standard let.

  • Completed lets, not lost deals. Turn a failed reference into a tenancy that proceeds, instead of starting the search again. The deal you had stays the deal you close.
  • Fast and simple. Refer through the portal, the tenant pays, the deed issues. It can be near instant, and you track every referral from Sent to Deed Issued in one screen.
  • Eligible for rent guarantee. With the opndoor guarantee in place, the tenancy is also eligible for a rent guarantee policy if the landlord wants one. An optional extra layer on top.
  • No cost to your agency or the landlord. The tenant pays the fee.

Who's a fit?

Likely a fit

  • Tenants in employment or on a stable income
  • Students
  • Tenants with solid savings
  • Some adverse credit can still be considered

Not a fit

  • Anyone who has misrepresented their application
  • Tenants with significant CCJs
  • Anyone with an IVA or undischarged bankruptcy
  • Rents above £10,000 a month

Every case is approved individually. Your account manager will walk your team through the detail.
