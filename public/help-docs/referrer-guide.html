Refer and track

Everything you need to send a referral, follow it through to the signed deed, and see how you rank. For everyone who refers through the portal.

What this is

When a tenant cannot pass referencing or provide their own guarantor, you can refer them to opndoor's professional guarantor service in a couple of minutes. opndoor provides a Deed of Guarantee in favour of the property so the let can go ahead instead of falling through.

This portal is where you send those referrals and track each one. You see your own referrals: every application you have sent, from Sent through to the signed deed.

At a glance
  • You refer a tenant who cannot pass referencing.
  • The tenant pays the guarantee fee agreed with your agency, usually one month's rent. On a joint tenancy it is split between the tenants.
  • The tenant signs their Deed of Guarantee, and you track it to completion.
  • You never enter the guarantee reference, issue date or expiry. They are assigned automatically.

Signing in with two-factor

The portal holds live tenant data, so every account signs in with two-factor authentication.

  1. Enter your work email and password.
  2. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
  3. The first time, you will scan a QR code to enrol your authenticator. Keep that app; you will use it every time you sign in.

Lost your authenticator? Ask your manager or opndoor to reset it, and you will set up a new one at your next sign in.

Sending a referral

Open New application and complete the sections in order:

  1. Tenant. Name, contact details and date of birth. Add each tenant on a joint tenancy.
  2. Property. The address being let.
  3. Tenancy. The monthly rent and the tenancy start date.
  4. Office. Which of your offices the let belongs to. The fee and the deed follow it, so it is worth getting right.

Press Send and the tenant gets a secure link to pay. Once they have paid, opndoor issues the deed for them to sign.

Where the offices come from

If you work at an agency opndoor has onboarded, the Office list is the offices opndoor has set up for you. A new office is added by opndoor rather than on this form, so ask your manager or your account manager if one is missing.

If you work at a supplier, you can add a new agency or office while you refer. The buttons sit under the agency and office pickers. A new agency needs its name, address and the email signed deeds should go to; a new office needs its name and address, and its email is optional. Your referral goes ahead straight away, and opndoor checks the new record afterwards.

Tracking your applications

The Applications screen lists your referrals. Each one moves through the same lifecycle:

StatusWhat it means
SentThe referral is with the tenant to pay the guarantee fee.
PaidThe guarantee fee has been paid, and the deed is being prepared.
Awaiting signatureThe deed is with the tenant to sign. On a joint tenancy each tenant signs their own.
Deed issuedThe Deed of Guarantee is signed and stored on the record, and a copy has been sent.
Withdrawn or expiredThe referral was withdrawn, or expired unpaid. Both are final.

Filter by status, agency or office, search by tenant or reference, and narrow to a time period. Open any row for the full detail, including the deed once it is signed.

Your League ranking

The League shows how you rank among referrers at your agency, by referrals sent in the period you choose. If your agency keeps the referrer table private, you will see your own performance rather than the full table.
