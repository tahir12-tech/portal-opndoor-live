What you get

opndoor acts as guarantor on your tenancy

Through a Deed of Guarantee in your favour, opndoor stands behind the rent. It is a professional guarantor service, giving you a clear, written guarantee to rely on rather than a deposit and a hope.

12

Up to 12 months' rent covered

You are covered for up to twelve months of unpaid rent, capped at £120,000, plus £10,000 of legal costs. Real protection if things go wrong.

How a claim works

Clear and straightforward

  1. Notify opndoor within two weeks of the second month of arrears.
  2. Make your claim to opndoor directly, or your agent can do it for you.
  3. Payments begin one month after eviction proceedings start, and continue from there.
Joint tenancies

On a joint tenancy, each tenant has their own deed covering their share of the rent. The £120,000 cap and £10,000 legal costs apply to the whole tenancy, split between the deeds in the same shares.
