What it is

opndoor acts as guarantor for your tenancy

A guarantor promises the landlord the rent will be covered. If you don't have one, opndoor can act as guarantor for you, so the tenancy you applied for can go ahead.

£

A one-off fee, usually one month's rent

Split between you if you're renting jointly. You pay it once to set up the guarantee, and there's nothing more to pay after that.

✓

You get a copy of the deed

Once the guarantee is issued, you receive a copy of the Deed of Guarantee for your records.

Being straight with you

What the fee is, and what it isn't

  • It is not rent, and not rent paid in advance.
  • It is not a deposit, and doesn't come off your rent.
  • It is a one-off guarantee fee for opndoor acting as guarantor.
  • It is what makes the tenancy possible when you can't provide your own guarantor.
One important thing

The guarantee protects the landlord, not you. You still pay your rent as normal. What it does for you is open the door to a home you'd otherwise miss out on.

Good to know
↺

Full refund if it falls through

If the tenancy doesn't go ahead, you get a full refund of the fee.

⚡

A decision first, then the fee

Your agent refers you, and opndoor decides whether it can stand as your guarantor. You are told either way. Only if you are accepted are you sent a secure link to pay, so you are never asked for the fee before you know you have the guarantee.

Take your time, then decide

If this feels like the right way forward, your letting agent can talk you through the next step. There is nothing to pay while opndoor is deciding. Take the time you need and ask anything you're unsure about.
