Before you refer

Referral information checklist

Everything the New application form asks for, in the order it asks. Gather it before you start and a referral takes a couple of minutes.

The tenant

  • Title — required.
  • First name and last name — required. Their legal name, as it appears on their ID.
  • Middle name — optional.
  • Date of birth — required.
  • Email — required. This is where the payment link goes, so check it with them.
  • Phone — required.

The property

  • Address line 1, city or town and postcode — required. You can search by postcode and pick the address.
  • Address line 2 and county — optional.

The tenancy

  • Monthly rent — required. The rent for the whole property, not one tenant's share.
  • Tenancy start date — required. It can be changed later, up to the day the tenancy starts.

Who it is for

  • The agency and the office — required. Start typing and pick from the list.

If more than one tenant is renting together

Add each tenant and give each one a share. The shares have to account for the whole rent between them, and each tenant pays their own part of the fee and signs their own deed. You need the full set of tenant details above for every one of them.

One thing worth checking twice

The tenant's email and their legal name. The email is where the payment link goes, and the name is what the deed is issued in.
